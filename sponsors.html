Who I Am

I'm Jeremiah Townsend, a small hunting content creator filming my own DIY public-land hunts for elk and mule deer across Wyoming and Colorado. Sometimes I hunt solo, sometimes with my dad, and sometimes with my wife — always on foot, always self-filmed. No guides, no big production — just the real hunt, misses and long pack-outs included.

Content I Make

  • Full hunt films: self-filmed elk and mule deer hunts, start to finish.
  • Gear in the field: pack lists, rifle and bow setups, and what held up out there.
  • Scouting and e-scouting: big-mile scouting trips, glassing, and finding country.
  • Shorts, Reels and TikToks: teasers and moments from the hill, including the Wyoming mule deer hunt.
